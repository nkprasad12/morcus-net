# PHI Classical Latin Canon Survey & Expansion Roadmap

**Measured:** 2026-11-07
**Morcus baseline:** `f96aa1bf` (`318` Library works / `6.47M` total words in [`library_constants.ts`](../../library_constants.ts), of which `~5.91M` words belong to the PHI canon and `~565k` words are post-200 AD `stoa*` / Justin works outside PHI)
**Scope:** Complete **Packard Humanities Institute (PHI) Latin Texts** online canon (`https://latin.packhum.org/canon`: **363 author/corpus entries, 837 works, ~7.98M words**—comprising the **362 classical `LATxxxx` authors / 836 works / ~7.31M words** plus **`PHI 4004` _Biblia Sacra Vulgata_ / ~674.5k words** from `CIV0004`)
**Context:** see [README.md](README.md), [CORPUS_SURVEY.md](CORPUS_SURVEY.md), [EXCLUSIONS.md](EXCLUSIONS.md), and [`upstream_sources.lock.json`](../../../../../upstream_sources.lock.json).

> [!IMPORTANT] > **Combining existing open digital corpora (~1.76M missing words) with public-domain (`<= 1930`) critical editions directly extractable from the PHI 5.3 CD-ROM (~127.3k–132.1k net-new words across 305–306 works) gives Morcus ready, copyright-clean digital sources for ~7.80M of the ~7.98M words in the entire online PHI Canon (97.7%)—or ~8.36M total Latin words including non-PHI works already in Morcus.** Only **156 PHI works (~183.6k words, 2.3% of the PHI canon)** remain in post-1930 copyright on the PHI CD without an open-corpus digital text, and **64.7% of that entire residual (~118.8k words)** sits in just three prose works: Quintilian _Declamationes Minores_ (64.5k), Hyginus _Fabulae_ (28.8k, Rose 1933—entering US public domain Jan 1, 2029), and Scribonius Largus (25.5k).

> [!CAUTION] > **Two Copyright / Edition Traps When Onboarding Open-Corpus HTML**:
>
> 1. **`thelatinlibrary` Vulgate (`raw/bible/`)**: Although `work_index.json` in [`nkprasad12/TheLatinLibrary`](https://github.com/nkprasad12/TheLatinLibrary) marks `ll/biblia-sacra-vulgata-novum-testamentum` (134,973 w) and `ll/biblia-sacra-vulgata-vetus-testamentum` (539,481 w) as `"status": "candidate"`, **18 of the 49 Old Testament files have zero punctuation because they were copied from Robert Weber's copyrighted Stuttgart Vulgate (1969/1983)** (`baruch`, `chronicles2`, `ecclesiastes`, `esther`, `ezekiel`, `ezra`, `isaiah`, `jeremiah`, `job`, `judith`, `lamentations`, `nehemiah`, `proverbs`, `psalms`, `sirach`, `songofsongs`, `tobia`, `wisdom`). Source the Vulgate from a clean public-domain **1592 Clementine Vulgate** repository instead.
> 2. **Unnamed `thelatinlibrary` Texts in Row 1A Above**: For the **41 TLL works in Row 1B (~134.7k words)**, prefer extracting directly from the **PHI 5.3 CD** (`phi-public-domain-json`) so Morcus gets named pre-1931 critical editions (Goetz-Schoell, Goetz, Clark, Vollmer, Maurenbrecher) and clean citations. Conversely, for TLL works in **Row 1A** where PHI 5.3 digitized a post-1930 edition (e.g., Goold 1985 Manilius, Mynors 1964 _Panegyricus_, Hellegouarc'h 1982 Velleius, Ranstrand 1971 Mela), do **not** take them from the PHI CD, and spot-check TLL's unnamed HTML against pre-1931 print scans (Housman, Baehrens, Shipley/Ellis, Frick) per [EXCLUSIONS.md](EXCLUSIONS.md).

## 7. Word-Count, Canon & Edition Verification Methodology

> [!NOTE] > **No text contents were scraped or downloaded from `latin.packhum.org`.** Because `latin.packhum.org` is gated by Cloudflare and the PHI 5.3 CD-ROM files are not checked into this repository, the entire canon inventory, edition audit, and word-count model were reconstructed deterministically from **four open-source metadata/index repositories on GitHub** combined with **direct word counts from local open-corpus clones**.

### 7.1 How the Canon & Verbatim PHI 5.3 Editions Were Reconstructed

1. **Classical `LAT0002`–`LAT9969` Registry (362 Authors, 836 Works)**: Extracted from [`cltk/tlg_indices`](https://github.com/cltk/tlg_indices) (`phi5_authors.py` and `phi5_works.py`), which parses the `AUTHTAB.DIR` / `LAT9999.TXT` canon directory from the PHI 5.3 CD-ROM.
2. **Auxiliary `CIV0004` / `PHI 4004` (_Biblia Sacra Vulgata_)**: On the physical PHI 5.3 CD-ROM, 7 auxiliary files (`CIV0001`–`CIV0007`: Hebrew Bible, LXX, Greek NT, Latin Vulgate, English KJV, RSV, and Penn-ujen morphological data) sit alongside `LATxxxx.TXT`. Querying Wikidata (`P6941` PHI author ID & `P8311` PHI work ID) and the Digital Latin Library catalog confirmed that `latin.packhum.org/canon` exposes exactly one `CIV` file (`CIV0004` -> `PHI 4004.001` _Biblia Sacra Vulgata_), bringing the online canon to **363 author/corpus entries and 837 works**.
3. **Verbatim `LAT9999.TXT` Source Editions (`publishedYear <= 1930` Audit)**: The Digital Latin Library Catalog open-data repository ([`DigitalLatin/linked-open-data`](https://github.com/DigitalLatin/linked-open-data), `csv/web-pages-export.csv`) contains 836 records pointing to `http://latin.packhum.org/loc/<author>/<work>/0`, each preserving the verbatim `Source Edition` string from `LAT9999.TXT` (e.g., `M. Tulli Ciceronis Scripta Quae Manserunt Omnia. Fasc. 1, ed. F. Marx, 1923` for `0474.073`, or `The Minor Declamations Ascribed to Quintilian, ed. M. Winterbottom, 1984` for `1002.002`). Joining those 836 records (+ `CIV0004` Weber 1969/1983) 1-to-1 against our 613 missing/partial works yielded exact publication years for every missing work.

### 7.2 How Word Counts Were Obtained Without PHI Text Files

Every missing/partial work's word count (`Word-Count Basis / Notes` in §§3–5) comes from one of three sources, in order of precedence:

1. **Direct Token Counts from Local Open Corpora (~1.42M words across ~90 works)**:
   - Wherever a missing work exists in full in local [`nkprasad12/canonical-latinlit`](https://github.com/nkprasad12/canonical-latinlit) (`phi1014.phi002` Seneca the Elder _Excerpta_ = `21,747` w), [`nkprasad12/TheLatinLibrary`](https://github.com/nkprasad12/TheLatinLibrary) (`work_index.json` + `raw/*.html` across ~87 classical works/sections and the Vulgate = `~1.11M` w), [`curran-gehring/modern-douay-rheims`](https://github.com/curran-gehring/modern-douay-rheims) (`vulgata_clementina_verses.tsv` = `642,303` w across 73 books), or [`diyclassics/lat_text_tesserae`](https://github.com/diyclassics/lat_text_tesserae) (Servius _In Georgica_ = `48,500` w, Ennius _Annales_ = `5,300` w), Latin word tokens were counted directly after stripping XML/HTML markup, notes, and headers.
2. **PHI 5.3 CD-ROM `LATxxxx.TXT` Byte-Size Calibration (`pjheslin/diogenes`)**:
   - Peter Heslin's open-source Diogenes repository ([`pjheslin/diogenes`](https://github.com/pjheslin/diogenes), `server/Diogenes/phi_files.txt`) checks in the exact byte size of all 363 `LATxxxx.TXT` files on the PHI 5.3 CD-ROM (stored in multiples of Ibycus's `8,192`-byte `[8 KiB]` blocks).
   - Calibrating `LATxxxx.TXT` byte size against Morcus's exact `latin_corpus.json` word counts across **23 complete single-source PHI authors (`>= 32 KiB`)** already in Morcus establishes a tight empirical conversion ratio:
     - **Verse authors**: `7.10 – 7.49` bytes/word (**median `7.27` B/word**; e.g., Vergil `7.34`, Lucan `7.27`, Lucretius `7.10`, Juvenal `7.20`, Silius `7.19`, Valerius Flaccus `7.27`).
     - **Prose authors**: `7.40 – 8.19` bytes/word (**median `7.70` B/word**; e.g., Caesar `7.40`, Quintus Curtius `7.47`, Vitruvius `7.55`, Celsus `7.54`, Ammianus `7.61`, Pliny the Elder `7.89`, Gellius `7.88`).
     - **Whole-canon mass balance check**: Across all 362 `LAT` authors (`56,573,952` raw bytes, or `~54.47 MB` after subtracting `~4,096 B` average half-block tail padding + `~1,800 B` Ibycus header overhead per author file), Morcus's `5,909,500` present words + `1,400,673` estimated missing classical words = **`7,310,173` total classical words** (**`7.45` B/word overall**, within `< 1%` of the empirical median).
   - For single-work missing authors (`>= 16 KiB`—such as Zeno `303,104 B` -> `~39.6k` w, Festus `286,720 B` -> `~37.5k` w, Hyginus _Fabulae_ `221,184 B` -> `~28.8k` w, Scribonius Largus `196,608 B` -> `~25.5k` w, Terentianus Maurus `131,072 B` -> `~17.1k` w, Lucilius `81,920 B` -> `~10.3k` w) and residual works on partially covered multi-work authors (e.g., Quintilian _Declamationes Minores_ = `2,326,528 B` total Quintilian file minus _Institutio Oratoria_ [`172,226` w] and _Declamationes Maiores_ [`67,015` w] -> `~64,500` w), this byte calibration yields word counts accurate to roughly `±5–8%`.
3. **Sub-Author Page-Span Apportionment & Single-Block (`8,192 B`) Fragment Estimates**:
   - Because Ibycus stores every `LATxxxx.TXT` file in `8,192`-byte (`8 KiB`) multiples, any fragmentary author with `< 900` words occupies a single `8,192`-byte file block, and multi-work authors share a single `LATxxxx.TXT` byte budget.
   - For multi-work authors not in `TheLatinLibrary` (e.g., Servius `2,850,816 B`, Porphyrio `598,016 B`, Hyginus Gromaticus `106,496 B`, Varro fragments), the author's calibrated word budget was apportioned across sub-works using standard-edition page spans (Thilo-Hagen, Holder, Keil _GL_, Thulin) and known verse/fragment counts.
   - For the 439 Tier-4 fragmentary works (`< 500` words), individual estimates use _Oxford Latin Dictionary_ (_OLD_) citation/line maximums from [`PerseusDL/catalog_data`](https://github.com/PerseusDL/catalog_data) (`citecoll/Latin.csv`) and fragment counts in Ribbeck, Peter, Morel, Funaioli, and Malcovati. Individual Tier-4 fragment counts are rounded approximations, but their combined total across all 439 works is strictly bounded by their `8,192`-byte file blocks at **~58,600 words (2.8% of missing text)**.

## 8. Implementation & Onboarding Playbook

For anyone picking up this workstream, each acquisition path plugs into an existing stage of [`processLibrary()`](../../process_library.ts) with specific conventions and a few generalizations needed before bulk-importing:

### 8.1 Path A — PHI 5.3 CD Public-Domain Extraction (`nkprasad12/phi-public-domain-json` -> [`process_phi_json.ts`](../../process_phi_json.ts))

- **How it works today**: `processPhiJson()` walks `$PHI_JSON_ROOT` and converts every `lat<authorCode>.<workCode>.json` file conforming to `RawPhiJson` (`author`, `title`, `authorCode`, `workCode`, `publishedTitle`, `editor`, `publishedYear`, `text: [[id4Tuple, line], ...]`) into a `ProcessedWork2` with work ID `phi-json-lat<authorCode>.<workCode>`.
- **Works that drop in with zero code changes**: 3-level prose works (`Book.Chapter.Section`) such as **_Rhetorica ad Herennium_ (`lat0474.073`, Marx 1923, ~31.3k w)**, **Varro _De Lingua Latina_ (`lat0684.001`, Goetz-Schoell 1910, ~40.5k w)** and **_Res Rusticae_ (`lat0684.002`, Goetz 1929, ~35.5k w)**, **Columella _De Arboribus_ (`lat0845.001`, Lundström 1897, ~6.0k w)**, and **Pomponius Porphyrio (`lat1512.001`–`006`, Holder 1894, ~78.5k w)**.
- **Three generalizations needed in [`process_phi_json.ts`](../../process_phi_json.ts) before bulk-extracting all 403 PD works**:
  1. **Variable Citation Depth (`textParts`)**: `convertRawPhi()` currently hardcodes `textParts = ["Book", "Chapter", "Section"]` and `convertPhiId([a, b, c, d]) -> [a, d, c]`. Many PHI works have 1- or 2-level hierarchies (leaving outer Ibycus tuple slots empty or `"1"`). Extend `RawPhiJson` with an optional `textParts?: string[]` (or infer active citation levels per work) so 1- and 2-level works divide cleanly.
  2. **Verse & Fragment Line Preservation**: `preprocessPhiText()` currently groups lines by `(id[0], id[3], id[2])`, strips trailing hyphens (`-‐‑`), and joins `id[1]` lines into prose paragraphs. For verse works (_Ilias Latina_ `lat0890.001`, Terentianus Maurus `lat1518.001`, _Priapea_ `lat0965.001`, Publilius Syrus `lat0646.001`) and verse fragments (Lucilius `lat0097.001`, Ribbeck _TRF_/_CRF_, Morel _FPL_), `id[1]` **is** the verse line number and must be kept as the leaf citation level rather than collapsed into prose.
  3. **Editorial / Witness Headers in Fragment Collections**: In Marx, Ribbeck, Peter (_HRR_), Morel (_FPL_), and Funaioli (_GRF_), fragment headers or ancient witness attributions should either be stripped or emitted as `kind="editorial"` spans (matching `EditorialSegment` in [`process_thelatinlibrary.ts`](../../process_thelatinlibrary.ts)) so witness labels are visible in the reader without polluting corpus search.
- **Machine-Readable Allowlist**: A pre-built allowlist of all **403 public-domain (`<= 1930`) missing/partial PHI 5.3 works** (with pre-populated `authorCode`, `workCode`, `author`, `title`, `publishedTitle`, `editor`, and `publishedYear` fields ready for an Ibycus -> `RawPhiJson` extractor) is saved in `.investigation/phi-canon-survey/phi_cd_pd_allowlist.json` (and can be regenerated from `DigitalLatin/linked-open-data` `csv/web-pages-export.csv`).

### 8.2 Path B — Promoting `TheLatinLibrary` Works ([`src/scripts/thelatinlibrary/onboard.ts`](../../../../scripts/thelatinlibrary/onboard.ts))

- **How it works today**: All ~90 missing PHI works in `TheLatinLibrary` are already checked into `$THELATINLIBRARY_ROOT/raw/` and indexed in `$THELATINLIBRARY_ROOT/work_index.json` (where they currently surface in the **Experimental Texts** catalog via [`catalog_builder.ts`](../../../../scripts/experimental_texts/catalog_builder.ts)).
- **Promotion workflow**: To promote a Row 1A work (where the PHI CD edition is post-1930 ©, e.g., Manilius, Velleius, Frontinus, Hyginus _Astronomica_, Apuleius philosophical works, Pliny _Panegyricus_, Cicero _De Legibus_, Mela, Fronto letters) into the main Morcus Library and Corpus:
  1. Add a `WorkSpec` entry to [`src/scripts/thelatinlibrary/onboard.ts`](../../../../scripts/thelatinlibrary/onboard.ts) using the HTML converters in `src/scripts/thelatinlibrary/converters/` (e.g., `bracketed_chapters.ts`, `declamation.ts`).
  2. Run `npm run tsnp src/scripts/thelatinlibrary/onboard.ts -- <workId>`, which writes `$THELATINLIBRARY_ROOT/works/<workId>.json` and cross-checks token overlap against `cltk/lat_text_latin_library`.
  3. `processTheLatinLibrary()` automatically ingests every `works/*.json` file into the main Library and Corpus, and `loadPromotedLatinLibraryWorkIds()` in [`catalog_builder.ts`](../../../../scripts/experimental_texts/catalog_builder.ts) automatically removes promoted IDs from Experimental Texts.
- **Partial-work stitch — Columella Books 10–12 (`PHI 0845.002`)**: Morcus already serves Columella Books 1–9 via Perseus (`phi0845.phi002.perseus-lat3`). When onboarding Books 10–12 (~31.4k w; note Book 10 is hexameter verse while Books 11–12 are prose; Books 10–11 are Lundström 1902–06 [PD on PHI CD] and Book 12 is in TLL), either append Books 10–12 into the Perseus TEI / V2 work or onboard them with a clear title (`De Re Rustica, Books 10–12`) so readers can navigate all 12 books.

### 8.3 Path C — Onboarding the _Vulgata_ (`PHI 4004.001`), Commentaries (Servius & Porphyrio), and TEI-XML

- **_Biblia Sacra Vulgata_ (`PHI 4004.001`, ~642.3k–674.5k words)**:
  - [`nkprasad12/modern-douay-rheims`](https://github.com/nkprasad12/modern-douay-rheims) (`data-sources/Vulgata_Clementina/vulgata_clementina_verses.tsv`) provides all 73 canonical books of the public-domain 1592 Clementine Vulgate in a single clean `book | chapter | verse | text` TSV (`642,303` words).
  - **Pagination tip**: Convert to `TheLatinLibraryWork` / `ProcessedWork2` shape with `textParts: ["Book", "Chapter", "Verse"]` and **`pageDepth: 2`** (matching how `thelatinlibrary-justinian-digesta` paginates by Digest title), so each reader page is one Biblical chapter (`Gen. 1`, `Matt. 5`, etc., ~1,334 chapter pages total) rather than 73 massive full-book pages or 35,000 single-verse pages.
- **Servius (`PHI 2349.005`–`007`, ~358.0k w) & Porphyrio (`PHI 1512.001`–`006`, ~78.5k w)**:
  - Both are public domain on the PHI 5.3 CD (Thilo-Hagen 1878–1902 and Holder 1894), and Servius is also in [`nkprasad12/lat_text_perseus`](https://github.com/nkprasad12/lat_text_perseus) (`/Vergil/opensource/serv.*.xml`) with explicit `<lemma>` tags.
  - **Reader feature opportunity**: Because Morcus already serves all of Vergil and Horace via Hypotactic, preserving the target poem/book/line in Servius and Porphyrio row IDs enables a future cross-link between Vergil/Horace lines and their ancient scholia.
- **Seneca the Elder _Controversiae excerpta_ (`PHI 1014.002` / `phi1014.phi002.perseus-lat1`, ~21.8k w)**:
  - Already present in `$LIB_XML_ROOT` ([`nkprasad12/canonical-latinlit`](https://github.com/nkprasad12/canonical-latinlit)) AND public domain on the PHI 5.3 CD (Müller 1887). Tracked under Group B in [EXCLUSIONS.md](EXCLUSIONS.md); onboarding the Perseus XML requires fixing the upstream `subtype="chaper"` typo and supporting ragged citation depth ([PLAN.md](PLAN.md)).
- **Zeno of Verona _Tractatus_ (`PHI 2150.001`, ~39.6k w)**:
  - Already present in `$PATROLOGIA_LATINA_ROOT` ([`nkprasad12/patrologia_latina-dev`](https://github.com/nkprasad12/patrologia_latina-dev), _PL_ vol. 11) and surfaced in Experimental Texts; can be promoted directly from the _PL_ TEI-XML.
